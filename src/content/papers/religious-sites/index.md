Does a temple nearby make a thief hold back? Matching hundreds of thousands of theft cases in China with the locations of Buddhist temples and Taoist shrines nationwide, this paper finds that thefts committed closer to religious sites, or where such sites are more common, are less severe, and that this effect weakens in more developed regions.
