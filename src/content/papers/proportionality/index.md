Must punishment that fits the crime cost us deterrence? After China softened its harsh sentences for embezzlement and bribery in 2016, the number of these crimes rose, but the rise came almost entirely from minor offences, and offenders had less reason to escalate to serious ones. Proportionate punishment and deterrence turn out to go together.
