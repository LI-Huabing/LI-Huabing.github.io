Hidden fees and endless product suggestions push online shoppers into choices they would not otherwise make, but what do they actually cost? In an experiment in a mock online store where both money and time run short, this paper measures how much these two tricks take from shoppers, in money and in time, and whether more vulnerable shoppers lose more.
